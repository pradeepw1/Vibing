# Lotería

A Lotería game you can play in the browser. Open `index.html`. It's one file, so there's nothing to install.

- **Play on this screen:** add up to 6 players. Everyone gets a random 4×4 board. Flip cards one at a time, tap a picture to put a bean on it, and the first to finish the pattern wins. Then hit **Rematch** for a new shuffle.
- **Just call the cards:** the screen becomes the deck, for paper boards or friends on their phones. Tap **¡Lotería!** to see every card called so you can check the winner.
- **One board:** a single board for your phone.

Pick how to win: a line, four corners, or the full board. The caller can read each card's name (or its rhyme too) out loud, and **Auto** flips a card every 4, 6 or 9 seconds.

The pictures are emoji plus two hand-drawn ones (the flag and the harp), not the printed card art. Two cards from the classic deck are swapped for El Elote and La Piñata.
